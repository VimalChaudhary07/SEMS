| Shared-component golden tests | 35 component fixtures, each snapshotted in light and dark themes (70 baselines). Full screen-by-screen golden comparison remains pending in Phase 11. |

These components are available from individual files in
`lib/shared/widgets/`; `sems_widgets.dart` continues to export the new
components alongside the existing legacy heading/metric/chart wrappers.
Component-level baselines do not mean whole-screen visual parity has been
signed off.
